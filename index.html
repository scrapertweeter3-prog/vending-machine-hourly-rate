The second vending machine pays for the first

A used combo machine runs about $1,800. Stock it at $2.25 a vend with $0.80 of product inside, and 85 vends a month clears about $123 after goods. The cost that kills single machines is the drive: one restock a week, 24 miles round trip at 35 cents a mile all-in, 65 minutes door to door. Four trips a month is $33.60 of car and 4.3 hours of time, so the real rate lands at $20.69 an hour.

Break-even at that rate takes about 20 months: $1,800 against $89.65 a month of net payback, and one soft month stretches it further. That rate is why so many single-machine routes get abandoned before the box is paid off.

A second machine in the same break room changes the denominator. Margin doubles to $246.50 a month, service time grows 15 minutes to 5.3 hours, and the car cost stays $33.60 because the trip is the same trip. Net $212.90 over 5.3 hours is $39.92 an hour, and the second box pays for itself in about 8.5 months. The machine is the same; the hourly rate nearly doubles.
